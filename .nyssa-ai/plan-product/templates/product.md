# Product

## Purpose

Describe what the product is, who it serves, and why it exists in plain, non-technical language.

## Audience

List the primary users and the jobs they need done.

## Problem

Describe the pain, gap, or workflow failure this product addresses.

## Solution

Describe the product shape and the smallest useful version from the user's perspective.

## Boundaries

List non-goals, constraints, and things this product should not own.

## Success

List the observable user outcomes that show the product is working.
